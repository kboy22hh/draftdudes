Our Mission

Built on
Film, Not
Hype

DraftDudes started as a spreadsheet and a group chat. We were tired of draft coverage that either parroted ESPN consensus or manufactured controversy for engagement. We wanted a platform built around what actually matters — process, film, and honest evaluation.

Every prospect on our board has been watched, graded, and debated. We don't finalize a ranking until we've disagreed about it at least twice. That friction is the product.

We're independent, unsponsored, and fully accountable to the tape.

What We Stand For

The
Standard

01
Film First

Every grade starts with the tape. Combine numbers and measurables are context, not conclusions.

02
Independent

No affiliations, no sponsors, no pressure to align with mainstream boards. Our rankings are ours.

03
Accountable

We publish our grades and stand behind them. When we're wrong, we say so and explain why.

04
Data-Backed

Our models don't replace scouting — they pressure-test it. Every projection has a methodology.
